DripMusic is a performance-focused fork of the Material 3 YouTube Music client Metrolist.

Features:

- Play any song or video from YT Music
- Background playback
- Personalized quick picks
- Configurable home screen: toggles for every section plus a speed dial grid, and a master toggle that hides all YouTube recommendation sections (home rows, mixes, similar-to, moods & genres) including their network requests
- Smooth scrolling and fast loading, thanks to fork-specific performance fixes
- Library management
- Download and cache songs for offline playback
- Search for songs, albums, artists, videos and playlists
- Live lyrics
- YouTube Music account login support
- Syncing of songs, artists, albums and playlists, from and to your account
- Skip silence
- Import playlists
- Audio normalization
- Adjust tempo/pitch
- Local playlist management
- Reorder songs in playlist or queue
- Light - Dark - black - Dynamic theme
- Sleep timer
- Material 3
- etc.

Based on Metrolist by MetrolistGroup, licensed under GPL-3.0.
